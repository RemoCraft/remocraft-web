Ores — Mineral Economy

Ores is a progression-focused gamemode where mining, refining and trading minerals is the core loop. Move from safe mining to high-risk PvP zones to obtain top-tier resources.

Overview

In Ores players start in a protected spawn area called "Las Palmas" where basic mining and economy happens. As players upgrade their shards and gear, they venture into the wild to find full ore blocks and face PvP competition.

Progression & Tiers

Resources are collected as Shards that can be combined to upgrade tiers (e.g. 4x Tier 1 = 1x Tier 2). Higher-tier shards unlock stronger equipment upgrades and crafting recipes.

Zones

  • Safe Zone (Las Palmas): Protected area for initial mining and trading.
  • Wild / PvP Areas: High-risk zones with richer resources and full mineral blocks. PvP is enabled.

Events

The main recurring activity is the hourly KOTH-style "Capture-the-Node" event. Teams or solo players fight to control the node and secure large rewards.

Economy & Shops

A spawn market allows players to buy and sell resources. The economy supports player-run shops and a balanced flow of resources between miners and PvP-focused players.

Style & Theme

The Ores mode follows a Japanese-inspired visual and narrative theme across UI and cosmetics.

Quick Tips

  • Start refining shards early to climb tiers faster.
  • Use spawn shops to liquidate surplus shards before risky runs.
  • Coordinate KOTH attempts with your team/clan for consistent rewards.
