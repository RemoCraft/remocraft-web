Server Rules

This ruleset replaces the old format and is intended to be clearer, more consistent, and easier to apply. Repeated sanctions that do not themselves carry a larger penalty may be cumulative, and repeated misconduct can lead to more severe action depending on the player's history.

Important: if repeated conduct continuously harms the experience of other players, staff may apply stronger sanctions than the base penalty for a single warning.

  1. Hack / cheat use
    • Any unauthorized modification to gain unfair advantages, such as X-ray, fly, speed hacks, PvP autoclickers or other cheats, is strictly forbidden.
    • If we detect someone using hacks and they deny it: 15-day ban, with 30 days possible in severe or repeated cases.
    • If they admit it when questioned: 7-day ban, with 30 days possible in severe or repeated cases.
    • If they confess on their own or are identified without being confronted: 7-day ban, potentially increasing to 30 days if it is repeated.
  2. PvP abuse and unfair mechanics
    • TPAKILL: killing someone after they accept a /tpa or /tpahere is not allowed. This also covers coordinated situations where one player accepts the teleport and another player kills them.
    • SPAWNKILL: repeatedly killing someone near their spawn, bed, or respawn point to prevent them from playing is prohibited. Exceptions may exist if the affected player is violating rule(s), but staff decides case by case.
    • 3 vs 1: ganging up on a single player is prohibited unless there was a previous agreement, a land war, or clear provocation. The goal is to prevent large groups from killing the first person they see.
    • Disconnecting during PvP: disconnecting mid-fight to avoid losing is punishable.
    • PvP when it is disabled: intentionally attacking another player when PvP is off for that player is sanctionable.
    • Robbery when PvP is disabled: if two players are not in active PvP, you cannot take advantage to steal their items or resources unfairly.
  3. Language, insults and offensive content
    • Very offensive language including racist, sexist, homophobic, xenophobic or other discriminatory remarks is strictly forbidden. Even if framed as a joke, it is not acceptable.
    • Basic insults like "idiot", "moron", "retard" or similar may result in a warning and short mute, escalating if repeated.
    • Political discussion is not appropriate on the server.
    • Religion is allowed in a roleplay or historical sense, but it is not allowed to pressure others into joining or following a religion.
    • Explicit sexual content or similarly provocative messages are punishable with warning, mute or ban depending on the situation and repetition.
    • Message instigation: repeated threats, taunts, or baiting by messages can escalate from warning to ban.
  4. Chat and behavior
    • Spam: repetitive useless messages in chat can lead to warnings and mutes.
    • Flooding: sending the same letter, word or pattern over and over.
    • Constant use of caps: occasional caps for emphasis is fine, but writing almost everything in uppercase is not.
    • Talking about other servers: advertising or discussing other servers in the chat is not allowed.
    • Toxic behavior: repeated harassment, taunting after a kill, mocking a defeated player, scams or any conduct that repeatedly harms others.
  5. Skins, nicks, builds and advantages
    • Inappropriate skins or nicknames, including extremist, sexual or offensive ones, are not allowed.
    • Impersonating staff is prohibited. Using a similar nickname is not a problem if no one is being tricked, but intentionally abusing the illusion of staff authority will be punished.
    • Inappropriate builds, explicit pixel art or content that disrupts the server experience are punished with warnings and possible stronger action if repeated.
    • Items with offensive names are not allowed.
    • Abusing VIP benefits after VIP has ended or without proper access can result in removal of the benefit and a warning or temporary ban.
    • Lag machines: deliberately causing lag with machines or structures is penalized; if it is reported and removed promptly, staff may only warn and clean the machine.
  6. Bugs, exploits and economy abuse
    • Abusing bugs or exploits to gain resources, advantages or unfair benefits must be reported before it is exploited. Sanctions may range from removal of the gain to a 1–7 day ban depending on severity.
    • Abusing lag or server mechanics without authorization may lead to warnings, removal of the machine and, if repeated, a stronger penalty.

Toxic behavior and cumulative sanctions

Toxic behavior includes any conduct that repeatedly harms, intimidates or annoys others, even if it is not a direct PvP violation. If a player accumulates multiple warnings or minor sanctions without a larger sanction, staff may increase the final penalty progressively based on the player's history.

  1. Repeated harassment: chasing or attacking the same user repeatedly to prevent them from playing normally.
  2. Taunting after a death: mocking a defeated player in an obvious way, especially if it is repeated.
  3. Harassment by the player who died: responding with insults, taunts or repeated stalking.
  4. Scams: deceiving players into giving up items, money or other valuable resources.
  5. Message instigation: provoking a fight or death through repeated messages or private messages.
  6. Repeated disconnections during PvP or abuse of the fight to avoid consequences.

Buying VIP ranks does not exempt you from following the rules. If you break a rule, you will receive the same penalty as any other user, and staff may apply cumulative consequences based on prior incidents.

If you have read the rules, you can go and enjoy the fun on our server!
